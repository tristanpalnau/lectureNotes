How to prove two propositional logic statements are 
equivalent?

two truth tables - show that for every possible input,
    the statements have the same output

deduction - statement1 |- statement2
    AND statement2 |- statement1


How to prove propositional logic statements are 
NOT equivalent?

    find ONE truth assignment where the outputs differ 

can't prove not equivalent with deduction




What is satisfiability? 
    I can find at least one truth assignment that makes
    a statement output true 

    don't need a full truth table, but could

To show NOT satisfiable?
    need to complete full truth table
    would be contradictory


filling out truth tables
    mark top-level operators
    fill in under each operator 
    classify it at the bottom
        contingent - give truth assignments 



- Determining whether an argument is valid or invalid
    (what is an argument?)

    argument: set of premises, a conclusion 
        show: premises -> conclusion


    valid: 


    how prove valid?
        truth table: find all truth assignments
            where EVERY premise is true, make sure
            the conclusion is also true 

        could also use deduction:
            premises |- conclusion 

    how prove invalid?
        find one truth assignment where all premises are
        true but the conclusion is false

        can't use deduction in this case
