Suppose you have the following propositional atoms:

p: It is sunny tomorrow
q: I will go hiking
r: I carry an umbrella
s: I am tired
t: I eat an apple


Express each of these propositional logic statements in English as
succinctly as you can.


Statement 1: p → ¬r ∧ q

If it is sunny tomorrow, then I won't carry an umbrella and I will go hiking.


Statement 2: ¬(r ∧ t) → ¬q

If I don't both carry an umbrella and eat an apple, then I won't go hiking.


Statement 3: s → (¬p → ¬q)

If I am tired, then, if it is not sunny tomorrow, I won't go hiking.
