We have the following set of students:

--------------------------------------------------------|
Name    | Major     | KS resident   | Lives on-campus   |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Bob     | PHYS      | True          | False             |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Ava     | ECE       | True          | True              |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Jane    | CS        | False         | False             |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Steve   | MATH      | True          | True              |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Marie   | CS        | False         | True              |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Jack    | ECE       | True          | False             |
--------------------------------------------------------|

And the following predicates over our set of students:

IsCS(x) : whether student x is a CS major

InKS(x): whether student x is a Kansas resident

Campus(x): whether student x lives on campus



Consider the following predicate logic statements:

1) ∀ x (¬InCS(x))

    a) Express the statement in words

        All students are not CS majors


    b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.

        False -- Jane is a CS major.

///

2) ∃ x (¬Campus(x) ⋀ InKS(x))

    a) Express the statement in words

        There is a student who doesn't live on campus and is a Kansas resident.


    b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.

        True -- Bob lives off campus and is a Kansas resident.

///

3) ∀ x (¬Campus(x) → InCS(x))

    a) Express the statement in words

        All students who live off campus are CS majors.
        (Or, all students who don't live on campus are CS majors)

    b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.

        False -- Bob lives off campus and is a PHYS major

///

4) ¬(∃ x (Campus(x) ⋀ InCS(x)))

    a) Express the statement in words

        There does not exist a student who both lives on campus and is a CS major.

    b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.

        False -- Marie lives on campus and is a CS major.


///

5) ¬(∀ x (InCS(x) → Campus(x)))

   a) Express the statement in words

    Not all students who are CS majors also live on campus.


   b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.

   True -- Jane is a CS major who lives off campus.
