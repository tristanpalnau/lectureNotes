Use a direct proof to prove the following proposition:

If x and y are even integers and z is an odd integer, then xyz is even.

///

Proof. 

Let x and y be even integers and let z be an odd integer. Then x = 2k, y = 2m,
and z = 2n+1 for some integers k, m, n. We have that:

    xyz = (2k)(2m)(2n+1)
        = 4km(2n+1)
        = 8kmn + 4km
        = 2(4kmn + 2kmn)

Thus xyz = 2a, where a is the integer 4kmn + 2kmn. It follows that xyz is even.

(Note: it is OK to rewrite xyz in the form 2(2km(2n+1)) instead.)
