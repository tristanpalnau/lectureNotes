Use a direct proof to prove the following proposition:

The product of two odd integers is odd.

///

Proof. Let x and y be odd integers.
Since x is an odd integer, there exists an integer k such that x = 2k + 1.
Since y is an odd integer, there exists an integer m such that y = 2m + 1.

We have that:
    xy = (2k + 1)(2m + 1)
        = 4km + 2k + 2m + 1
        = 2(2km + k + m) + 1

Thus xy = 2b + 1, where b is the integer 2km + k + m. We have that xy is odd,
by definition of an odd number.
