Predicate logic:

Instead of propositions, we will make claims about
individuals in a domain (a set)


These claims will involve predicates and quantifiers

domain: all living things

Human(x) - whether x is human
Mortal(x) - whether x is mortal


∀ x (Human(x) → Mortal(x))

For all living things, if that living thing is human, then it is mortal.

All humans are mortal.
