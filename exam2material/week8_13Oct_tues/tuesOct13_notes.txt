Reminder:

AllE - when can we use?
    need a for all statement 
    individual of that type 
        might be a parameter to proof function (or named in a premise)
        might be individual named inside a let subproof


AllI - when do we use? idea?
    when our goal is a for all statement 
    subproof (a "let" subproof where we name a random individual, like a)
        goal: get our statement about that individual
    afterward, make same claim with a for-all statement (must be true
        for everyone in domain)
